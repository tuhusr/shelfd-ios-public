This is a tracker app / website like IMDb, Letterboxd, and MyAnimeList. It allows you to track movies, TV shows, anime, books, rate everything, track episode progress, comment on media, share media and lists, with a very heavy emphasis on the social / friend aspect.
Track Everything
Shows, movies, games, episodes, and progress.
Rate & Discuss
Rate titles, seasons, episodes, and comment with friends or globally.
Discover With Friends
Add friends, compare lists, and find what to watch next.

• If you are on mobile - I recommend opening on SAFARI and clicking "Add to Home screen" for the most optimal presentation and use.

• I am based out of NY and it is where I currently live. In my account page on the site, you can see my social exports. This is so you can see I am a real normal human being. Just a guy who wanted to fix his own issue with no proper app visually and functionally to track tv show episodes Updates will be constant and on the way. Bear with me as I get this up and running for an app. I am very thankful for all the feedback and trust me there is much more to come!

• YES this will very soon be an app. That is the most requested thing and is my top priority.

• The userbase has been steadily going up and I am glad that a side project that was for personal use has become a main stay for so many people

• I am the solo developer and creator with a small team of my friends who are on my creative team. Because of the nature of the website, login is required for it to function properly. Preview mode was causing too many bugs.

• This app/website at its core is a media tracker (like IMDb, Letterboxd, MyAnimeList, AniList) with a very heavy emphasis on social features and user profile expression.

• The website is completely free, and I made it originally for personal use. My friends were amazed at the build of the site and also wanted to use it, and that is what led me to making it an available website for people.

• Still a work in progress – I LITERALLY just started this project on April 26th, 2026.
