Frequently asked

What is Shelfd?

Shelfd is a personal tracker for movies, TV shows, anime, video games, and music — combined with a friend-first social layer. You build a shelf of everything you've watched, played, or listened to; rate it, tier-list it, and see what your circle is into.

How do I add something to my shelf?

Tap the + button in the bottom navigation, search for what you want, choose the media type, pick a status (Watching, Watched, Watchlist, Paused, Playing, Backlog, etc.), and tap save.

How do tier lists work?

Open any category (movies, TV, anime, games, or music), tap Tier List, and drag titles into S-, A-, B-, C-, D-, E-, or F-tier. Tier lists are saved separately from your personal star ratings, so you can experiment without changing your shelf.

What is the Rating Game?

The Rating Game shows you two titles from your library side-by-side and asks which you prefer. After enough rounds, your true favorites surface based on actual head-to-head comparisons — not just your raw star ratings.

How do competitive games work?

When you add a game and set its status to Competitive, you unlock a deeper profile with peak rank, current rank, lifetime K/D, season K/D, hours played per platform, and a slot for your stats tracker link and highlight reels.

How do I delete my account or my data?

Email shelfd@proton.me with the subject "Account deletion request" from the email address tied to your account. We'll confirm and permanently remove your account and library data within 7 days.

How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Email shelfd@proton.me. Screenshots and the version number (Settings → About) make bugs much faster to fix.

What devices does Shelfd support?

Shelfd runs on iPhone (iOS) via the App Store, and on any modern browser at myshelfd.com. Android and iPad versions are on the roadmap.

Is Shelfd free?

Yes — the core experience is free. A future Pro tier will unlock advanced analytics, deeper stats, and exclusive features. Existing features stay free.
